## Authority and assurance contracts

- Historical observations/findings remain with #46/#47 and their domain methods.
- Method truth remains with #60; a case-derived method is not promoted by fluent prose.
- Accepted requirements remain with #42.
- Reversible technical choice/delivery remains with #48/#59.
- Deterministic validators may check form, references, transitions and changed-code
  trace coverage, never historical truth, method sufficiency or owner value.
- The research owner may accept goal/value/workflow usefulness, not substitute for
  specialist historical validation.
- AI can inspect, structure, research, propose and implement reversible mechanics.
  It cannot silently accept domain semantics, requirement deltas, owner value or
  consequential publication.

## Real findings only

1. **Missing end-to-end slice record.** The Moxa work can be reconstructed only by
   joining owner comments, research artifacts, requirements and generic assurance
   records. No canonical record links the research pain/finding to a specific accepted
   requirement, technical decision, implementation, verification and owner outcome.
2. **Candidate disposition debt.** `RC-U2-01..24` are correctly not auto-promoted,
   but the reviewed state does not give each candidate a visible
   `fuse/refine/reject/defer/remain-case-specific` outcome.
3. **Method maturity is honestly partial.** #60 correctly records that concrete
   domain profiles are not yet sufficiently operationalized. This is a real gap,
   not permission for a technical substitute.
4. **Derived-view and validator duplication is already friction.** #64 and
   `FB-20260901-002` identify navigation/maintenance load and duplicate CI work.
   Consolidation is justified only when the next real consumer appears.
5. **Owner acceptance is not yet demonstrated for the research capability.**
   Issue checkpoints are valuable feedback, but are not a linked product-use
   acceptance record.

## Redundancies and unnecessary handoffs

- #46 → #60 → #42 → #48 → #59 → #63 is a valid authority path, but it need not be
  six manual handoffs for every excerpt. Source-local work should remain inside #46;
  only material method/requirement/technical deltas cross an authority boundary.
- `PROJECT_STATE.md`, responsibility maps and coverage views repeat navigation and
  status concerns. They should be derived or narrowly scoped, never manually maintained
  as parallel truth.
- Requirements and value-assurance validators duplicate loading, findings and CI
  plumbing. Extract shared utilities only when another real mechanism consumes them.

## Evidence-backed automation opportunities

1. Generate a bounded slice view from existing canonical references:
   driver → requirement → decision → implementation → verification → feedback.
2. Flag requirement candidates without explicit disposition after review, without
   deciding the disposition.
3. Compile a work context from owner/artifact pointers and report missing inputs.
4. Generate derived navigation/coverage views from canonical data.
5. Share validator loading/reference/finding utilities when the next validator needs
   them; keep existing commands as thin interfaces.
6. Route owner feedback to an open delta when the outcome is
   `pain-persists | regression | new-pain | new-need | requirement-change`.

## Non-changes

This pilot does not change Arnshaugk, its requirements, method truth, findings,
owners or architecture. It does not claim that the Moxa slice is fully productized.
It preserves the observed gap as an acceptance/regression fixture rather than
inventing a framework to hide it.
