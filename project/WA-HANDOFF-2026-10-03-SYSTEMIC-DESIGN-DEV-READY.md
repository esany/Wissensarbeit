## 10. Definition of DEV READY for the coming systemic revision

Do **not** use `DEV READY` to mean “we have many findings”.

The revision is `DEV READY` only when:

1. problem/root-cause scope is sufficiently bound;
2. existing Requirements are dispositioned and material changes separately authorized;
3. design-relevant external research is sufficiently complete;
4. materially different concept directions have been compared, including simpler/no-new-structure options;
5. one system design has been selected with evidence and explicit non-goals;
6. ownership/interfaces/authority/current-vs-history/learning implications are resolved;
7. acceptance/eval/failure conditions can falsify the design;
8. migration from the actual current repo is understood;
9. independent design review positively confirms the frozen design or its corrected successor;
10. implementation work can be derived without making developers rediscover material architecture/semantics;
11. remaining uncertainty is implementation-detail uncertainty rather than hidden material design choice.

Until then: no systemic implementation admission.
