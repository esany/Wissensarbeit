# 12. Kill criteria / evidence required before reopening a new Bridge hypothesis

A distinct Bridge/Interface capability becomes materially plausible only if repeated evidence shows all of the following pattern:

1. Human Intent/Erkenntnis meaning is correctly reconstructed and source-role safe.
2. BB-COMPETENCE correctly detects the relevant capability need.
3. Existing Capability-Request semantics are correctly applied.
4. Existing Skill/Composition execution is correct within its contract.
5. Existing Result-Envelope semantics are correctly returned.
6. Existing Integration/Reconciliation/Assurance mechanisms operate correctly.
7. **Despite 1–6, an action-relevant semantic distinction is repeatedly lost specifically at the boundary.**
8. Contract sharpening does not remove the failure.
9. Composition of existing owners does not remove the failure.
10. The failure repeats in materially different projects and yields a stable reusable purpose/failure boundary.

Without this discrimination, a new Bridge risks duplicate ownership, pipeline hardening, ontology/orchestrator creep and meta-system accretion.
