## 5. Frozen discrimination cases

The cases below are frozen for this comparison before assigning the final class. They combine current repository semantics with adversarial counterfactuals. Designed cases are evaluation stimuli, not claims of real runtime behavior.

### Case A — inbound discrimination under the same superficial capability label

Superficial label: `analysis / research`.

Three materially different needs are intentionally given the same superficial label:

- **A-K Knowledge Gap**: external/domain evidence is required; closure is epistemic evidence, not Human acceptance.
- **A-U Understanding/Judgement Gap**: the Human must be able to understand/assess the result; source/evidence/uncertainty and later Human validation matter even if no extra external research is required.
- **A-S System/Capability Gap**: the question is whether observable system behavior exists; documentation or a research report cannot close the gap.

Material differences that must survive handoff:

- current Intent / purpose;
- gap/epistemic need where supported;
- evidence/quality depth;
- constraints/non-decisions;
- authority/validation boundary;
- required observable closure condition.

Failure signal: the three cases collapse into the same Skill choice/output/STOP behavior merely because the surface label matches.

### Case B — outbound partial result attribution

A correct bounded Skill result establishes one evidential finding that reduces a Knowledge Gap, but:

- does not establish Human understanding/judgement;
- does not establish a System/Capability behavior;
- does not authorize Requirement/architecture/promotion;
- leaves another relevant semantic relation unresolved.

Failure signal: the result is converted into whole-state closure, or Integration must reconstruct from scratch which semantic obligation changed.

### Case C — multi-Skill composition

Three individually bounded results concern the same material question:

- Skill result R1 supports a candidate relation with medium uncertainty;
- R2 contradicts the relation with stronger direct evidence;
- R3 is compatible with both but leaves a separate obligation unresolved.

Failure signal: composition resolves by recency, majority, model prestige or a hidden central narrative instead of preserving provenance, contradiction, uncertainty and independent completion state.

### Case D — NOW/DONE distinction

Real-evidence anchor: the P2 Context-Fidelity history used in #54 C1.

A bounded implementation/task may exist and formal assurance may be green while qualitative usefulness/material completeness/Human acceptance remain open; later independent review can still find material defects.

Required distinction:

1. bounded task/Skill completion;
2. observable System/Capability availability;
3. information/result availability;
4. sufficient Human understanding/judgement;
5. Human acceptance/material validation.

Failure signal: the system must collapse these states to operate or invent a new controller merely to keep them distinct.

## 10. Why a distinct Bridge is not supported now

A distinct capability would need repeated evidence of a stable transformation failure after:

- Human meaning is correctly bound;
- competence need is correctly detected;
- the sharpened Capability Request is correctly applied;
- the selected Skill(s) execute correctly;
- the sharpened Result Envelope is correct;
- existing Integration/Reconciliation/Assurance operate correctly.

The current cases do not provide such a residual failure.

Creating a Bridge now would therefore be driven by conceptual symmetry rather than demonstrated independent competence. That conflicts with #52 compose-before-create and the Governing Objective's simplicity/anti-meta-work constraints.
